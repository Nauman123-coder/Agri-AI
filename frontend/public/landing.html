Who We Are

Built for the Kisan,
not the corporation.

KhetAI was created with a singular mission: to give Pakistan's 8+ million small-scale farmers access to the kind of intelligent, automated decision-making that large agribusinesses take for granted. We are an AI-first agricultural platform that speaks your language — literally.

We understand that a wheat farmer in Sahiwal doesn't have time to research pesticide vendors, compare prices, or negotiate delivery schedules. That's why we built an autonomous agent that does all of that for you — transparently, quickly, and at no extra cost.

"Every farmer deserves the same quality of agricultural intelligence as a Fortune 500 agribusiness. KhetAI makes that a reality."

GAP
The Challenge

Pakistan's farmers face an
Advice-Action Gap.

Millions of farmers across Punjab and Sindh receive agricultural advice they cannot act upon. They know their crops are diseased. They know which pesticide they need. But they lack the time, connections, and negotiating power to secure the best deal. Knowledge without execution is lost potential.

🔍

Diagnosis Without Action

Farmers can identify crop diseases through extension workers or basic apps — but translating that diagnosis into a purchased solution takes days of effort, phone calls, and market visits that many cannot afford.

💸

No Negotiating Power

Individual small farmers purchasing pesticides or fertilizers in small quantities have virtually no leverage with vendors. They pay full retail price while large farms secure bulk discounts of 15–25%. KhetAI changes this equation.

⏳

Time Is the Harvest

Crop disease spreads exponentially. Every day spent searching for the right vendor, comparing prices, and arranging logistics is a day of escalating crop loss. Speed is not a luxury — it is survival for a subsistence farmer.

The Process

From photo to deal confirmed in minutes.

KhetAI's autonomous pipeline turns a single crop photo into a fully negotiated vendor agreement — with economic analysis, risk modeling, and parametric insurance assessment built in at every step.

📸

Photograph Your Crop

Open KhetAI on your phone and take a photo of the affected crop area. Our system accepts any standard mobile camera image and works even under difficult field lighting conditions. No special equipment is needed — just the phone in your pocket.

🔬

AI Diagnoses Disease & Severity

Our Grok Vision AI model analyzes your image and identifies the specific disease or pest infestation — from Fall Armyworm to Rice Blast — with confidence scores and severity ratings. You receive a structured report in both English and Urdu, with immediate action recommendations and a read-aloud voice option.

🎯 87% Accuracy

📊

Economic Loss Calculation

Our Monte Carlo simulation engine runs 10,000 economic scenarios based on your crop type, acreage, current market prices in PKR, and disease severity. You see exactly how much you stand to lose without treatment, how much treatment costs, and your projected return on investment — in plain language, in your currency.

📈 10,000 Simulations

🤝

Autonomous Vendor Negotiation

KhetAI's AI negotiation agent contacts multiple verified vendors across Punjab and Sindh on your behalf. It presents your requirements, negotiates for bulk discounts, evaluates delivery timelines, and returns a ranked list of offers with a clear recommendation. You see the entire negotiation conversation in real time.

Review the best deal and confirm with a single tap. Capabilities

Everything a modern
kisan needs.

Five integrated modules that work together to give Pakistan's farmers unprecedented control over their agricultural outcomes.

🧪

AI Crop Diagnosis

Upload any crop photo for instant Grok Vision analysis. Identifies 50+ diseases and pests with severity classification, confidence scoring, and multilingual reports in English and Urdu.

📉

Economic Risk Engine

Monte Carlo simulations calculate your net farm value, projected crop loss in PKR, treatment ROI, and best/worst-case scenarios — tailored to real Pakistan market prices and your specific acreage.

🤖

Autonomous Negotiation Agent

Our AI agent contacts verified vendors, presents your bulk requirements, negotiates discounts, and returns a ranked deal list. Watch the entire negotiation unfold in a real-time chat interface.

🗺️

Vendor Logistics Map

An interactive dark map showing verified agri vendors across Punjab and Sindh. Filter by pesticides, fertilizers, seeds, or premium suppliers. View cooperative aggregation zones for group purchasing.

🛡️

Parametric Insurance

Automatic insurance claim triggers based on disease severity thresholds — no manual inspection required. Two policy tiers covering pest damage, drought, flood, and market price drops.

🔊

Urdu Voice Interface

Every diagnosis, negotiation summary, and market alert can be read aloud in both English and Urdu using the Web Speech API. Designed for farmers who prefer listening over reading small text.
